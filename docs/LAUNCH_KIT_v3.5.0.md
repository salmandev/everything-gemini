## Executive Summary: An Entire Tech Company in Your Terminal

Building software with AI agents has reached its inflection point. You don't just need an AI that writes code—you need an **entire software enterprise** that:
1. **Scopes Strategy**: Sets MVP bounds, OKRs, and go/no-go gates (`ceo`).
2. **Defines Products**: Maps user journeys, authors PRDs, and specifies BDD criteria (`cpo`).
3. **Architects Systems**: Governs ADRs, system boundaries, and tech debt (`cto`).
4. **Enforces Security**: Exercises unilateral security vetoes against CVEs and leaks (`ciso`).
5. **Crafts UX/UI**: Generates semantic design tokens, responsive layouts, and WCAG AAA compliance (`ui-ux-designer`).
6. **Builds Systems**: Implements clean architecture, APIs, frontend, and databases (`fullstack-developer`).
7. **Certifies Quality**: Runs Playwright E2E suites and visual regression checks (`automation-tester`).
8. **Drives Growth & GTM**: Designs AARRR pirate funnels and viral loops ($K$-factor $\ge 1.2$) (`growth-hacker`).
9. **Positions the Brand**: Orchestrates multi-channel launch campaigns and DevRel positioning (`cmo`).
10. **Writes High-Converting Copy**: Crafts landing pages, launch kits, and CLI microcopy (`copywriter`).

**Everything Gemini v3.5.0** delivers this full 10-avatar autonomous organization for Google Antigravity and Gemini 3.8 across **164 test-verified components**.
